    Messy data · Claude3. Use the free Claude skill

    Give Claude your CMMS or MES export, even a messy one. It maps the causes to machines, builds a simulator page for your line, and lists every number it had to guess.

    Get the skill
    • Before you buy conveyor or a faster machine: see what it is worth in bottles first.
    • When people argue about the bottleneck: the simulator shows which machine everyone else is waiting on.
    • To justify a root-cause fix: turn the downtime log into bottles a shift, which is easy to put in dollars.
    • To train supervisors: starved, blocked and down are easy to understand once you watch them happen.
